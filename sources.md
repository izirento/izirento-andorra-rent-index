## Notes on sources

- Law 15/2022 of 23 May, consolidated text of the Urban Property Lease Law, as published in the BOPA and on the Consell General site. It was later amended (including Law 24/2023 and Law 5/2025). Only provisions whose core rule was checked as still in force on the check date are included; rent updates, temporary rent measures and penalties are left out because they have changed often.
